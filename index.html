01 The Short Version

What I've Done

Two engineering degrees, three startup co-ops, two peer-reviewed publications, and a consulting practice — all built around one idea: take things from bench to deployment. I fabricate the sensor, write the firmware, and train the model that makes sense of the data.

Education
University of Waterloo ×2
M.A.Sc. Mechanical & Mechatronics Engineering (2024–2025) on a foundation of B.A.Sc. Nanotechnology Engineering (2019–2024). Thesis work on novel thin-film semiconductor deposition for gas and humidity sensing.
Research
Published & Cited
First-author work in Small (CuOx thin films for humidity sensing) and Advanced Sensor Research (PEDOT:PSS hydrogels for flexible biosensing), plus a thesis on quartz crystal microbalance biosensing.
Industry
Startups & Semiconductors
Engineering co-ops at Kenota Health (YC) and Avro Life Science (YC) building point-of-care diagnostics and life-science instrumentation, and Teledyne DALSA doing cleanroom MEMS process development.
Now
Consulting & Building
Technical consulting for Scarborough Health Network, Mecka AI, and a stealth manufacturing startup. Side projects: Preceptra (AI training tool), a Pi-based travel router, and lab-automation software.
