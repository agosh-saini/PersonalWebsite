01 About

The Builder

Engineer and researcher at the intersection of hardware, sensing, and machine learning. M.A.Sc. in Mechanical & Mechatronics Engineering from the University of Waterloo, built on a foundation in Nanotechnology Engineering.

My work spans custom sensor fabrication, embedded firmware, and the software systems that take research from bench to deployment. I consult with early-stage companies on technical strategy and conduct independent research across materials science and biomedical engineering.

Outside the lab: Muay Thai, and building things that don't exist yet.
