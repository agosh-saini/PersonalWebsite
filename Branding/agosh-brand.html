01 Brand Foundation

The Monumental Scholar

The brand essence is rooted in the tension between academic rigor and visionary creation. It is the architect's blueprint combined with the painter's brushstroke. We aim for significance over accessibility.

05 Usage Guidelines

System Execution

Element Rule
Logo Clearspace Maintain a minimum clearspace equal to the height of the 'a' in the wordmark.
Typography Tracking Headline tracking should be set to -0.04em. Utility tracking at +0.2em.
Layout Composition Prioritize asymmetric balance and generous vertical whitespace.
Color Ratio Deep Stone should occupy 80% of any composition. Vermilion is for focal points.
